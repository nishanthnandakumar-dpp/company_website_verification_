Who we are

Traceyon is a financial technology company in Chennai, India, founded by Nishanth Nandakumar. We build quantitative research systems, data infrastructure and software that keeps its own evidence.

The founders

Nishanth Nandakumar

Founder & CEO

I started Traceyon building research software for finance, and the first lesson was humbling: almost every promising result I produced turned out to be an accident of the data. The hard part was never finding something that looked good. It was proving whether it was real.

So that became the work. Traceyon builds systems that keep their evidence, show their working, and stop when they are unsure, the kind of systems I would want to rely on myself. We move in small, deliberate steps, and we would rather report an honest negative result than an impressive one we cannot defend.

If that sounds like the way you work too, I would like to hear from you.

— Nishanth

Nanda Kumar Kannan

Co-founder

I have spent three decades building and running a printing business in Chennai, where quality is checked before anything leaves the press and a reputation is earned one job at a time. In that time I have seen plenty of ideas promise more than they could deliver.

What persuaded me about Traceyon was the opposite habit: a system that refuses to believe its own good news until it has been checked, and that records its failures as carefully as its successes. That is how I have always run my own work. It is why I chose to co-found Traceyon, and why I stand behind it for the long term.

— Nanda Kumar
